(01) — The story

I'm an event photographer first. That means I read rooms for a living — where the light is, who's about to laugh, which moment the client will need on Monday morning.

I'm Keona Ngai, a photographer based in Vancouver, British Columbia. My work lives in two worlds that have more in common than people think: brand and corporate events on one side, weddings on the other.

Both are live. Both happen once. Both are full of people who have put real money, time and heart into a few hours — and who need someone calm in the room making sure those hours are kept properly.

For brands, that means I shoot with the deliverable in mind: the recap deck, the sponsor report, the launch-night carousel. I've worked alongside marketing teams, agencies and event producers on launches, activations, conferences and galas across Metro Vancouver, and I know that “we need ten images by 9pm” is not a suggestion.

For couples, it means the opposite of a production. I work quietly, keep formals quick, and spend the rest of the day watching for the things you'll have missed — your dad fixing his tie, your friends losing it on the dance floor.

Either way, you get the same things from me: preparation, punctuality, a steady presence, and a gallery that arrives when I said it would.

— Keona
