Good to know

How fast do we get the photos?

Previews within 24–48 hours. Full event galleries in 3–5 business days; wedding galleries within 4 weeks.

Do you travel?

Yes. Metro Vancouver is included in every package. Anywhere beyond is billed at cost — mileage, ferries, flights and accommodation where needed.

Can we use the images for marketing?

Yes — web, social, PR and internal use are included. Paid ads and print campaigns are covered by an extended licence, quoted to fit.
